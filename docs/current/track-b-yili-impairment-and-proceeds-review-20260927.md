## Impairment facts

The impairment notice (physical PDF pages 1-3) reports CNY 2,460,781,900 of
H1 provisions (CNY 246,078.19 ten-thousand), described as non-cash. Its detail
includes credit impairment as well as asset impairment. The interim report
keeps those statement lines separate (physical PDF page 167, printed page 164):

| Consolidated H1 line | CNY |
|---|---:|
| Credit impairment loss | -4,906,718.67 |
| Inventory write-down and contract-performance impairment | -907,520,836.34 |
| Fixed-asset impairment | -1,740,517.69 |
| Goodwill impairment | -1,546,544,866.49 |
| Other asset impairment | -68,952.50 |
| Total asset impairment loss | -2,455,875,173.02 |

The notice's combined provision total reconciles, subject to rounding, to the
separate credit-impairment and asset-impairment lines. Do not label the full
CNY 2.4608 billion as the income-statement `asset impairment loss` line.

The notice attributes CNY 907.5208 million of inventory write-down mainly to
Ausnutria's milk-powder channel restructuring and inventory review, plus
finished/semi-finished products whose net realizable value fell below cost.
It attributes CNY 1.5465 billion consolidated goodwill impairment to
Ausnutria's operating loss following channel restructuring and lower expected
future milk-powder growth. The notice says these are non-cash and do not have a
material effect on current/future operations or cash flow; that statement does
not erase the separate evidence of weaker operations and reduced forecasts.

The interim-report goodwill note (physical PDF pages 143-144, printed pages
140-141) provides the accounting bridge and test inputs:

- Ausnutria goodwill before current-period impairment: CNY 5,134,369,525.51.
- Prior accumulated impairment: CNY 2,990,956,858.71.
- H1 consolidated impairment: CNY 1,546,544,866.49; accumulated balance after
  this charge: CNY 4,537,501,725.20.
- The impairment test's CNY 2,613,731,400 total includes minority interests;
  the report separately says CNY 1,546,544,900 affects the consolidated
  statements. These are different scopes, not additive consolidated losses.
- The reported test uses carrying amount CNY 9,527,731,400 and recoverable
  amount CNY 6,914,000,000 for the asset group. Management's forecast-period
  inputs are revenue growth 5.05%-7.16%, gross margin 37.53%-43.36%, and a
  10.27% discount rate; terminal revenue growth is 2.00%, gross margin 43.36%.
  These are management/valuation-provider assumptions, not independently
  verified normalized growth or equity value.

Research treatment: retain the impairment as adverse evidence for normalized
ROE, acquisition/capital-allocation quality, and thesis-breaker monitoring. Do
not mechanically add it back as a one-off. The notice links the charge to
inventory action and a lowered growth outlook; evidence does not establish how
much of the affected business economics will recover, nor the after-tax and
attributable earnings impact needed to calculate normalized ROE. The existing
M1 disposition `MATERIAL_REQUIRES_RECALCULATION` remains open; this note does not
complete a valuation or promote the case to `VALUATION_READY`.

## Proceeds project facts

The 2026H1 special report (physical PDF pages 2-3, 5 and 7) and interim report
(physical PDF pages 39-40, printed pages 36-37) support:

- Net proceeds: CNY 12,040,965,800; cumulative reported use: CNY
  10,899,673,500; H1 2026 use: CNY 135,255,832.46; proceeds-account balances
  total CNY 933,865,021.35 including interest. The account balance covers
  multiple projects and must not be attributed to the digital project alone.
- The `Digital Transformation and Information Upgrade` project has adjusted
  investment CNY 1,093,715,800, cumulative use CNY 935,206,500, H1 use CNY
  77,737,100 and reported progress 85.51%; reported unspent project allocation
  is CNY 158,509,300.
- The special report narrative says the expected-use date was extended from
  December 2024 to December 2027. A complete, exact-issuer CNINFO calendar-year
  2025 query (132 records across five pages; index SHA-256
  `25be88f33af9e9bbe110d506efab95e3d0eec9d3a1959d1c521d88f143c455e2`) found
  the 2025-04-30 project-extension announcement and board resolution, the
  2025-05-21 annual shareholders' meeting resolution, and the sponsor's
  verification opinion. These originals corroborate that the extension was
  submitted to and approved through the disclosed governance process; the
  special report says the board met on 2025-04-28 and that the relevant
  shareholder resolution passed. The extension announcement itself still gives
  `不适用` in the same project's expected-use-date table. The 2026H1 interim
  report also lists `不适用` while showing 85.51% complete and not concluded.
  Therefore approval of the stated December 2027 extension is supported, but
  the conflicting table fields remain unexplained; this is not proof of actual
  completion or of a later revised date.
- Disclosed project benefits are qualitative (consumer, marketing, channel,
  supply-chain and digital operations capabilities). No quantified incremental
  revenue, margin, cash return, or project-level payback is supplied in these
  cited rows. A separate AI/security upgrade project and the Duolun equipment
  project are distinct uses and should not be conflated with this project.

Research treatment: retain `REQUIRES_DECOMPOSITION` for capital allocation and
reinvestment returns. The 85.51% spending progress is not an ROI measure. The
date mismatch and lack of project-level quantified returns remain unresolved;
do not infer failure from delay alone or success from qualitative benefits.
