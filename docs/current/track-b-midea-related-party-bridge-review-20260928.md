## Unreconciled Consolidation and Bank Products

- Parent-only other receivables are CNY 31.751670bn at FY2025 and CNY 36.018093bn at 2026H1; consolidated other receivables are CNY 3.190591bn and CNY 3.172621bn. This large difference is consistent with substantial internal balances, but the retained evidence does not include counterparty-level elimination entries or subsidiary-side account mapping. Exact elimination by item is **not verified**.
- The FY2025 related-party schedule reports CNY 6.294127bn in other non-current and current portions for deposits / large-denomination certificates of deposit at Shunde Rural Commercial Bank. The FY2025 consolidated related-party note shows CNY 6.922056bn across currency funds and those asset categories. For 2026H1 the corresponding schedule amount is CNY 2.644483bn versus CNY 4.268837bn of monetary-product investment in the consolidated note. The retained records do not reconcile the differences by product, holder, accounting category, maturity, redemption restriction or pledge status.
- Total related-party schedule balances of CNY 34.521231bn (FY2025) and CNY 35.270876bn (2026H1) mix subsidiary funding, service balances and bank products. They cannot be equated to one consolidated balance-sheet line.

## Interpretation Limits

Four of the five large receivables are labelled non-operating funds transfers to subsidiaries. They are parent-only claims, not consolidated external cash, and must not be added to cash, netted against debt or attributed to industrial operations. The service-related item also has a subsidiary counterparty; without invoices, settlement evidence and elimination detail it is not established as external group working capital.

Bank deposits and certificates are not proven to be immediately liquid, unrestricted or attributable to industrial operations. The available records do not support industrial net cash / net debt, normalized owner cash flow, valuation or dividend capacity. No such calculations were made.

## Remaining Evidence Gaps

1. Counterparty-level elimination entries, subsidiary-side accounts and period-end settlement reconciliation.
2. Terms, interest, maturity, security, use of proceeds and enforceability for the large funding transfers.
3. Counterparty credit evidence and support for individual versus portfolio impairment treatment.
4. Product-level bank deposit schedules with holder, maturity, liquidity, pledge status and reconciliation to the consolidated related-party note.
5. A segment-level bridge separating industrial and finance-business cash, debt and internal funding.
6. Transaction-level mapping of opening balance, additions and repayments to cash-flow statements.

The retained public filing package was independently searched for these items.
The five parent-only counterparty matches are complete as a public evidence
result (`PARENT_ONLY_COUNTERPARTY_MATCH=COMPLETED`). Counterparty-level
elimination entries and product-level bank reconciliation were not found in the
retained public disclosures and are classified as unavailable external evidence,
not as an unresolved arithmetic task. They must not be reconstructed from the
parent/consolidated total difference.
