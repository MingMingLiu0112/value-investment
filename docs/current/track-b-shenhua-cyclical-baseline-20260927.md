Coal volumes/prices are from the annual report's operating overview (printed
pages 30-32); power indicators are in the operating section (printed pages
33-37); consolidated summary figures are from printed page 8. Segment profit,
capex, and depreciation are from the report's financial-statement segment
note, printed page 278. Figures retain their reported scopes and must not be
mixed as if they were one homogeneous series.

FY2025 report-segment capex is CNY 44,686m versus CNY 34,190m in 2024; the
report defines it as spending/costs on segment assets expected to be used for
more than one year. The operating-section capital plan is CNY 44.686bn actual
for 2025 (excluding mineral-rights spending) and CNY 38.023bn planned for
2026, with a separately disclosed CNY 1.893bn of 2025 mineral-rights-related
spending (printed pages 46-47). A plan is not actual expenditure; neither
segment capex nor depreciation separates maintenance from growth. Depreciation
is not a valid proxy for maintenance capex.

## Disposition

Do not average 2024 and 2025 profits and call the result normalized. The
FY2024-FY2025 adjacent financial comparison spans a material realized-price decline, has
restatement and acquisition-perimeter effects, and lacks the maintenance-capex
split needed to translate segment accounting profit into sustainable owner
cash flow. The separate 2014-2025 operational-observation package is evidence
that has been collected, not a comparable normalized-earnings series or
approved model input. The available evidence improves the company baseline
and identifies the required reconciliation work, but it cannot validly produce normalized Bear/Base/Bull,
dividend capacity, a buy price, or an investment action. No baseline snapshot
or workbook value is changed by this memo alone. `action=no_order`.
