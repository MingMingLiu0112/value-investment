Parent-company balances also differ materially from consolidated balances. For
example, parent-company monetary funds were CNY 23.178bn / 36.723bn at the two

3.173bn. These differences indicate that parent and consolidated items cannot
be added together; they do not by themselves identify the exact elimination or
the amount available to industrial operations.
