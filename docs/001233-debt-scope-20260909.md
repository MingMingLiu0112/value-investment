## Follow-up: payables, recourse and available cash

Read physical pages 175, 176, 198, 200, 201, 234, 235 from the same pinned PDF.

- Page 200 other-payables presentation 378047882.02 equals dividends payable
  371946668.00 plus other payables 6101214.02. Page 201 splits the latter into
  deposits 811841.38, accrued expenses 4287434.16, other 1001938.48. Interest
  payable is explicitly 0.00. Do not classify the dividend balance as borrowing

- Page 200 trade payables total 487992351.88 includes long-term asset purchase
  payables 323776578.17, goods 149757614.07, freight 4339105.05 and other
  10119054.59. The asset-purchase label alone does not prove a financing term;
  neither classify all as debt nor assert all interest-free without evidence.
- Page 198 retained bills 7829732.83 plus transferred digital receivable
  claims 4525948.00 reconcile exactly to the 12355680.83 other-current-liability
  subline on page 203. Page 235 describes transfers as endorsement and
  receivable transfer, with non-derecognition; it reports period transfer
  amounts, not necessarily the year-end exposure, so do not substitute those
  period flows into the balance-sheet sum.
- Pages 175-176 describe a separate bank-acceptance receivable-financing
  balance of 5480706.40 endorsed/discounted and derecognized, with transfer of
  principal risks and rewards. Do not confuse it with the non-derecognized
  balances or add it automatically to year-end debt.
- Page 198 lists monetary guarantees 40416821.87 and in-transit funds
  5248000.00. Their sum is 45664821.87. Deducting only these disclosed items
  from gross cash 3069495469.62 gives 3023830647.75, an arithmetic research
  subtotal, NOT a verified freely available cash figure. Availability and

The liquidity maturity table is unsuitable as a complete borrowing measure:
it includes trade and dividend obligations. A financing-only assessment must
separate borrowing/leases, operating settlement liabilities, recourse exposure
and uncertain contractual financing. Current evidence does not yet establish
all contractual terms or an independent complete-debt cross-check. Keep the
official quality gate blocked; do not request the user to manually approve it.
