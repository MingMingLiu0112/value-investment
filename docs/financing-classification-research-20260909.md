# Financing Classification Research

Reviewed through browser on 2026-09-09.

## Primary Standard-Setter Source Read

IFRS Foundation, IAS 7 overview:
https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/

The About section describes financing activities as changes in contributed equity and borrowings. It separately discusses noncash investing/financing transactions. Standard history identifies the January 2016 amendment requiring disclosures of changes in liabilities arising from financing activities and the May 2023 supplier-finance disclosure amendment.

This is a primary standard-setter overview, not the full paragraph-level standard. Its current version also describes later changes; those must not be backdated into historical report requirements. It is not automatic authority for a specific PRC-GAAP issuer or proof of that issuer's compliance.

## Research Implications

1. A financing cash-flow item is not necessarily a financing liability. Equity issuance, dividends and financing fees must not become ending debt merely because they appear in a financing section.
2. Successful table arithmetic does not establish debt classification. The observed Sun Paper mixed table and the dividends in China Merchants Port provide concrete issuer examples.
3. Borrowings, bonds, leases, concession payment obligations, pensions, earmarked funds and supplier financing need separate classifications and explicit current-maturity treatment. These are analytical design requirements, not a claim that the IAS 7 overview prescribes a valuation formula.
4. Keep original amount, unit, period, reporting scope, measurement basis and source page. Link valuation adjustments to an explicit policy; never infer them just from the word interest or a discounting calculation.

## Government-Hosted CAS 31 Text Retrieved

Subsequent browser inspection retrieved the full text titled CAS 31 Statement of Cash Flows on the Changzhou Municipal Finance Bureau website:
https://czj.changzhou.gov.cn/content/suitable/show?id=5172&catid=1224

Displayed publication date: 2007-03-21. This is a government-hosted reproduction, not the Ministry's original issuance instrument or proof of the complete subsequent amendment history.

Read clauses:

- Article 14 defines financing activities as changes in the scale and composition of enterprise capital and debt.
- Article 15 separately lists cash from investment contributions and borrowings, repayment of debt, distributions of dividends/profits or payment of interest, and other financing receipts/payments.
- Article 18 requires note disclosure of significant investing and financing activities without current cash receipts/payments that affect financial position or may affect future cash flows.
- Article 19 requires disclosure of cash-equivalent composition and large cash amounts unavailable to the parent or other group subsidiaries. Therefore a future cash-versus-debt safety measure must distinguish unrestricted available cash from the entire monetary-funds balance.

These actual domestic clauses support separating financing cash-flow categories from ending debt balances. They do not prescribe this project's interest-bearing-debt valuation definition, validate the total in any issuer's financing note, or establish the modern six-column reconciliation template's full regulatory history.
