## Unresolved scope

Page 233 reports bank borrowings and other of 61,080,579. The individually
disclosed short-term plus gross long-term borrowings total 60,989,864.
Difference: 90,715 thousand, or CNY 90,715,000. This is not explained merely by
the label 'other'. Text search did not locate the literal formatted amount
90,715; that negative search is not proof of absence. The financing-table note
mentions foreign-currency translation and bills that cannot be derecognized.
Their role in the closing difference requires direct reconciliation, not inference.

Page 133 also reports deposits and interbank placements of 134,110. Their scope
and treatment in consolidated financing risk must be reviewed, rather than
silently treating this mixed group as a manufacturing-only balance sheet.
The page 263 liquidity maturity table includes future interest, and must not
be substituted for carrying-value debt.

The page 233 table total 67,571,712 reconciles arithmetically. Its covered
financing liabilities are not yet established as the exhaustive interest-bearing
debt input for the quality model. Same-report reconciliation is not independent
source verification. No production fact or investment signal was promoted.

## Bills hypothesis checked

Physical page 188, note 4(b), reports endorsed or discounted bills outstanding
at 2025-12-31. The not-derecognized column contains commercial acceptance bills
14,281 and bank acceptance bills 2,567,443, totaling 2,581,724 thousand CNY.
This population combines endorsement and discounting. It does not identify the
90,715 thousand closing difference, and must not be added wholesale to debt:
doing so could mix trade settlement with financing and double count existing
borrowings. The note explains classification of some bills as receivables
financing, not a reconciliation of all financing liabilities. The difference
remains unresolved; the literal-amount search is not a completeness test.
